# CafeCoin System

Build Now. Pay Smart.

Welcome to the official CafeCoin ecosystem repository by CodeCafe.

CafeCoin is a flexible EMI and milestone-based payment support system designed to help startups, entrepreneurs, and businesses begin digital projects without heavy upfront financial pressure.

---

# What is CafeCoin?

CafeCoin is introduced by CodeCafe to simplify digital transformation through business-friendly payment structures for services such as:

* Website Development
* Mobile App Development
* E-Commerce Solutions
* Branding & UI/UX
* Digital Marketing
* Business Automation Systems

---

# Core Vision

The goal of CafeCoin is to help businesses focus on growth instead of delaying digital transformation due to payment limitations.

---

# Key Features

* Flexible EMI options
* Milestone-based workflows
* Startup-friendly onboarding
* Reduced upfront investment pressure
* Business-focused digital enablement
* Scalable payment structures
